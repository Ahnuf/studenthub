# StudentHub Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

---

# 1. Introduction

StudentHub is an AI-powered academic workspace designed to help university students manage their academic life, study more effectively, and collaborate with classmates through a personalized, course-centric experience.

Unlike traditional Learning Management Systems (LMS) or University Management Systems, StudentHub focuses entirely on the student's perspective.

Instead of forcing students to organize everything manually, StudentHub understands their academic context and adapts the experience accordingly.

---

# 2. Vision

To become the most intelligent academic workspace for university students.

StudentHub should reduce the friction students face in managing university life and allow them to focus on learning rather than organization.

---

# 3. Mission

Help students:

• Stay organized
• Study smarter
• Collaborate effectively

through intelligent automation and AI-powered assistance.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

University students use multiple disconnected tools to manage their studies.

For example:

• Google Calendar
• Notion
• WhatsApp groups
• Google Drive
• ChatGPT
• PDF readers
• Flashcard apps

Switching between multiple applications increases cognitive load, wastes time, and reduces productivity.

StudentHub brings these essential academic tools together into one personalized platform.

---

# 5. Target Audience

Primary Users

• Graduate students

Initial Market

Future Expansion

# 6. Product Philosophy

StudentHub is NOT:

❌ University ERP

❌ Moodle replacement

❌ Social media platform

❌ Job portal

❌ Marketplace

StudentHub IS:

✅ Academic workspace

✅ AI study companion

✅ Course-centric productivity platform

---

# 7. Core Product Principles

Every feature must satisfy at least one of these principles.

1. Manage

Help students organize academic life.

Examples:

• Dashboard
• Timetable
• Assignments
• Attendance
• GPA
• Calendar

---

2. Learn

Help students understand and retain knowledge.

Examples:

• AI Tutor
• Flashcards
• Quiz Generator
• PDF Chat
• Notes

---

3. Collaborate

Help students learn together.

Examples:

• Course Q&A
• Announcements
• Study Groups

---

# 8. Personalization

StudentHub understands the student's academic context.

After profile setup, the system already knows:

• University
• Program
• Current Semester
• Selected Courses

This information is used to personalize:

• Dashboard
• Course recommendations
• AI responses
• Assignment tracking
• Discussions
• Calendar

Students should never have to repeatedly provide information the system already knows.

# 10. Long-Term Vision

StudentHub should become the default workspace students open every day before they begin studying.

Instead of asking:

"What do I need to do today?"

the dashboard should already answer that question.

---

# 11. What We Won't Build

The following features are intentionally outside the scope of StudentHub unless our product vision changes.

- General social networking
- Public discussion feeds
- Job portal
- Marketplace
- Video calling
- University administration tools
- Student admission management
- Fee management
- Hostel management
- Transport management
